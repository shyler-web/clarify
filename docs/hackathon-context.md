# NVIDIA x Nebius Global AI Hackathon — Context

> Compiled 5 October 2026. Source: Tavily research across Devpost, Nebius, NVIDIA, and community listings.

## Overview

The **Nebius × NVIDIA Global AI Hackathon (2026 edition)** is a free, global developer competition inviting AI builders to create high-performance AI applications and agents using **NVIDIA open-source models hosted on Nebius infrastructure**. Event operations and submissions are managed on **Devpost**. The broader NVIDIA–Nebius strategic partnership (announced March 2026 — investment + cloud partnership) underpins this hackathon.

## Timeline

| Milestone | Date |

## Format & Location

- Primarily **virtual/online** with optional in-person meetups ("Builders & Brews").
- At least one in-person **Build Week** (Berlin, 18 Oct 2026) with mentoring, workshops, hacking time, and final demos.

## Registration & Eligibility

- Register and submit via Devpost: `nebiusglobalaihackathon.devpost.com`
- Entry is **free**.
- Eligible: individuals at age of majority, teams of such individuals, and organized legal entities.

## Tracks

4 official tracks + a bonus:
- Coding & Agentic Engineering
- Best Apps & Agents
- Personal AI
- Physical AI
- Bonus: **Best Use of Tavily**

## Prizes

- Total prize pool **exceeds $50,000**.
- Grand prize reported **$20,000**; $10K second, $6K third.
- Specialty awards (e.g., Best Use of Tavily), city winner awards (~$500), plus hardware (reported NVIDIA Jetson Orin Nano devices for some track winners).

## Submission Requirements

- Project must **run on Nebius Token Factory or Nebius AI Cloud**.
- Must use **at least one NVIDIA open-source model** (e.g., Nemotron).
- Working project + English submission + **working demo URL** + short public video (≤3 min).
- Public GitHub repo with README (per community guidelines).

## Credits & Resources

- Activate Nebius Token Factory credits with code **`NEBIUS-DEVPOST-GLOBAL26`** (via Nebius resources form).
- Additional credits via the Nebius Builders Program.
- Nebius AI Cloud GPU types: NVIDIA H100 (`gpu-h100-sxm`), L40S (`gpu-l40s-a`), presets from single to multi-GPU (e.g. `1gpu-16vcpu-200gb`, `8gpu-128vcpu-1600gb`).
- Community support via Nebius Discord and `dev.nebius.com`.

## Known Gaps

- No published detailed judging rubric or judge names.
- No explicit CUDA/cuDNN/Triton version requirements or recommended container images.
- Date ambiguity: one listing cites 15 Dec 2026 as an event date; Devpost lists judging through 15 Dec and a separate winners announcement ~11 Jan 2027.
